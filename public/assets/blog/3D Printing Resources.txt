Setting up and re-leveling Ender 5 S1 printer
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MhX9aAkVFqI
Before You Begin
Materials Needed
* Ender-5 S1 assembled
* A4 printer paper (or standard letter paper)
* Filament loaded (optional for final test print)
Part 1: Reset Printer Settings
1. Power on the printer.
2. From the touchscreen, open:
   * Settings
   * Advanced Settings
   * Reset Configuration
3. Confirm the reset.
This ensures you're starting from factory calibration values.
Part 2: Set Leveling Method
1. Open:
   * Settings
   * Leveling Method
2. Select the leveling mode recommended by the printer.
3. Return to the main menu.
Part 3: Home the Printer
1. Navigate to the leveling screen.
2. Press Return to Zero (Home).
3. Wait for the printer to move the nozzle to its reference position.
Part 4: Set Initial Nozzle Height
1. Place a sheet of A4 paper on the print bed directly under the nozzle.
2. Use the compensation/Z-offset adjustment controls.
3. Slowly lower the nozzle until the paper can still move, but you feel slight resistance.
Correct Paper Feel
✅ Paper slides with light friction
❌ Paper moves freely with no resistance
❌ Paper is trapped and cannot move
Part 5: Manually Level the Four Corners
The printer will move to each leveling point.
For each corner:
1. Move the paper beneath the nozzle.
2. Turn the corresponding bed-leveling knob.
   * Clockwise → lowers the bed
   * Counterclockwise → raises the bed
3. Adjust until the paper has the same slight drag as before.
Repeat for:
* Front Left
* Front Right
* Rear Right
* Rear Left
Part 6: Repeat Corner Checks
1. Return to the first corner.
2. Check all four corners again.
Because adjusting one corner affects the others, continue making small corrections until:
* All four corners feel the same.
* Paper friction is consistent everywhere.
Part 7: Run Auto Leveling
1. Open:
   * Auto Level (AUTO LVL)
2. Press Start.
3. Wait while the printer probes multiple points across the bed.
The printer will create a mesh that compensates for small bed irregularities.
Do not touch the printer during this process.
Part 8: Prepare for Z-Offset Calibration
After auto leveling finishes:
1. Return to the main screen.
2. Open:
   * Prepare
3. Set the Z-axis value to 50 mm.
This creates room to safely heat and prepare the printer.
Part 9: Heat the Nozzle
1. Open:
   * Prepare
   * In/Out
2. Set nozzle temperature to:
   * 200°C (for standard PLA)
3. Wait until the temperature is reached.
Part 10: Start a Test Print
1. Select a simple test print (such as a first-layer square).
2. Begin printing once the printer reaches temperature.
Part 11: Fine-Tune the Z Offset During Printing
Watch the first layer carefully.
Nozzle Too High
Signs:
* Filament doesn't stick
* Lines are round and separated
Action:
* Lower the Z-offset slightly (more negative)
Nozzle Too Low
Signs:
* Filament is squished excessively
* Surface looks rough
* Nozzle drags through plastic
Action:
* Raise the Z-offset slightly (less negative)
Correct Height
Signs:
* Smooth lines
* Adjacent lines touch
* Good adhesion
* Consistent surface appearance
Adjust the Z-offset in very small increments (typically 0.02–0.05 mm at a time).




Fixing write-only protection on SD card
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=htpQlKpjbNw
1. Plug the SD card into your computer.
2. Press the Windows Key + R, type cmd, and hit Enter.
3. Type diskpart and press Enter.
4. Type list disk and press Enter to find your SD card's number based on its size.
5. Type select disk # (replace # with your SD card number) and press Enter.
6. Type attributes disk clear readonly and press Enter.
7. Safely eject the card, reinject the card to put your model on the card
8. Safely eject the card again, then try inserting it into the printer to see if the models pop up
